Naturally, most of your choices resulted in your own death. </p> <p> There are some <a href="http://samizdat.cc/cyoa/">really awesome visualizations</a> of <em>Choose your Own Adventure</em> stories out there. </p> <p> In computerized versions of decision trees, there is a data structure that works well. It breaks the decisions down into individual <strong>nodes</strong>. These nodes will be things like using a key or picking up a book. Each node will have certain possible actions attached to it. Each of these actions will link to a new node, with it's own actions and links. </p> <p> As the adventure starts, it begins with <code>node.start</code>: </p> <pre><code>start: { description: "You see a door.", choices: { 'Open the door': 'openDoor', 'Stay here and do nothing': 'stayHere' } },</code></pre> <p> Opening the door will triggerv<code>node.displayNode( node.openDoor );</code>. Staying and doing nothing will trigger <code>node.displayNode( node.stayHere );</code> </p> <hr /> <h1>The scary door!</h1> <h2>A choose your own adventure</h2> <hr /> <div id="descriptionId"></div>
